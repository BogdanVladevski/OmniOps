# Security policy

OmniOps stores companies' delivery records, customer details, documents and invoices. Reports of vulnerabilities are
taken seriously and handled promptly.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please **do not open a public issue**. Report privately through
[GitHub security advisories](https://github.com/BogdanVladevski/OmniOps/security/advisories/new) and include:

- what is affected (endpoint, screen, component) and the impact you see,
- steps to reproduce or a proof of concept,
- any suggested fix.

You can expect an acknowledgement within three working days and an assessment within ten. Fixes for confirmed issues
are released as soon as they are ready, and reporters are credited unless they prefer otherwise.

## Scope

Of particular interest: one company reaching another company's data, bypassing a role (e.g. a driver reaching office
functions), abuse of public tracking or invoice links, authentication and session handling, and stored content
(uploads) served back to users.

## Supported versions

Only the latest release on `main` receives security fixes.
